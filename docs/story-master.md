# 1. CREATIVE DIRECTION

## Logline

Sheru is a 22-year-old young man living in Mahe and helping his mother run a small juice shop. He is funny, stubborn and has a habit of saying exactly what comes to mind. One day, he discovers that the father he believed was dead is alive and was once the underworld king of Kochi. After his father's death, Sheru must face Vikram Singh, who is trying to seize Rajan's empire. A juice-shop boy begins the journey that will make him the King of Kochi.

## Core promise

At the beginning, Sheru's world consists of a juice blender, a delivery bag and customers who never pay on time. By the end, people across Kochi will turn around when they hear his name.

Sheru must not suddenly become an expert fighter, businessman or underworld leader. The player must feel his growth.

- He learns customer handling at the juice shop.
- Buying supplies and managing orders teaches him about stock, supply and delivery.
- Dealing with locals teaches him how to read people.
- At the beginning, he does not even know how to handle a gun.
- He makes mistakes, gets scared and gets angry, but never abandons his people.
- Even when he becomes rich, his relationship with his mother does not change.

## Tone balance

- **Mahe:** 70% comedy, 30% emotion.
- **Early Kochi:** 50% comedy, 50% discovery and tension.
- **Mid-game:** 35% comedy, 65% crime drama and action.
- **Final act:** 20% comedy, 80% emotion, tension and payoff.
- **Ending/free roam:** Comedy and warmth return.

Do not force jokes into every scene. Let characters react after a good joke. Reduce comedy during Rajan's death, the mother's pain and Bheeshma's injury.

## Central theme

**A title can be inherited. Respect must be earned.**

Rajan built an empire but could not live with his family. Vikram will do anything to gain power. When Sheru inherits his father's empire, he must decide whether to repeat the same mistakes or create his own path.

# 2. MAIN CHARACTER BIBLE

## SHERU BHAI — Protagonist

**Age:** 22  
**Home:** Mahe  
**Occupation at the beginning:** Helps his mother run a juice shop  
**Personality:** Sarcastic, funny, impulsive, loyal, street-smart, confident in conversation but inexperienced in organised crime.

Sheru is a good person, but that does not mean he speaks sweetly to everyone. If someone annoys him, he says what comes to mind. He roasts his friends and gets irritated by customers' ridiculous demands. He is still a little afraid of his mother.

### Dialogue personality by story stage

- **Mahe:** Quick comebacks, local slang, teasing, occasional swearing and plenty of confidence.
- **Early Kochi:** He is overwhelmed by the new world. He questions Bheeshma. Guns, luxury cars and the mansion are all unfamiliar.
- **Mid-game:** His confidence grows. His jokes become sharper and he learns to read people.
- **Late game:** He speaks calmly and threatens people only when necessary. His humour remains part of his personality.

## RAJAN BHARATHAN / RAJAN BHAI — Father

**Age:** Around 58  
**Role:** Former King of Kochi.

Rajan controlled a large business network and underworld influence in Kochi. The empire was built on harbour operations, transport, warehouses, properties, finance and loyal people.

When Sheru was young, Rajan left after the family was threatened. He believed distance would protect them. But it was also a failure as a father. He watched his son from afar but could not bring himself close.

Do not portray Rajan as a perfect hero. He loved his family, but he also made the wrong decision.

## BHEESHMA — Rajan's right-hand man

**Age:** Around 55–60  
**Role:** Rajan's trusted lieutenant for 30 years; Sheru's mentor.

Bheeshma is calm, disciplined and experienced. He knows Rajan's people, enemies, businesses and history.

Before dying, Rajan asks Bheeshma to protect Sheru and teach him how the empire works.

**Relationship arc:**
1. Calls him “Young Master”.
2. Struggles to tolerate Sheru's jokes and carelessness.
3. Recognises his intelligence and good heart.
4. Protects him like a son or a member of his own family.
5. At the end, calls him simply “Sheru”.

Bheeshma should rarely joke. Sheru's humour becomes more effective when Bheeshma stays serious.

## JOMON — Childhood friend

**Age:** Around 22  
**Home:** Mahe  
**Role:** Sheru's closest friend and later companion.

Jomon loves talking, free food and announcing grand plans. He is usually the first to get scared when danger arrives, but he stands by Sheru when it truly matters.

He must not be just a comic character. His courage and loyalty should grow by the end of the story.

## VIKRAM SINGH — Main antagonist

**Age:** Around 45  
**Design:** White tiger  
**Role:** Rajan's former close associate and current rival.

Vikram helped build Rajan's empire and was once his trusted associate. A dispute over control and the future of the empire separated them. Vikram believes Rajan denied him the power and recognition he had been promised.

His old grievance may be genuine, but it does not justify his present cruelty and greed for power.

## SHERU'S MOTHER

**Age:** Around 45–50  
**Role:** Runs the juice shop in Mahe.

Sheru's mother is loving, practical and strong. She knew Rajan was alive but hid the truth from Sheru to protect the family. Keeping that secret was never easy for her.

She does not care about Sheru's money or status. She cares whether he has eaten, slept and cleaned the shop.

Mahe residents should remember Sheru as the juice-shop boy. Kochi residents should gradually recognise his name as his reputation grows.

# 4. WORLD AND CONTINUITY RULES

## Mahe

The opening area should include:
- Mother's juice shop.
- Grocery shop.
- Tea shop.
- Mechanic workshop.
- Residential lanes.
- Local market.
- Delivery destinations.
- Waterfront or beach.

The player learns the town through ordinary errands before the crime story begins.

## Kochi

The larger city includes:
- Rajan's mansion.
- Harbour and port operations.
- Warehouses.
- Transport yards.
- Commercial districts.
- Nightlife areas.
- Residential neighbourhoods.
- Vikram-controlled properties.
- Sheru's later headquarters.

Use fictional businesses and fictionalised locations inspired by Kerala.

## Five pillars of Rajan's empire

1. **Harbour:** Workers, access and logistics.
2. **Transport:** Vehicles, drivers, depots and delivery contracts.
3. **Finance:** Accounts, debts, investments and business records.
4. **Properties:** Warehouses, offices, buildings and land.
5. **People:** Loyal employees, associates, contacts and community relationships.

Sheru must rebuild these pillars through missions. He does not receive automatic respect because he is Rajan's son.

## Continuity decisions used in this draft

- Rajan was once Vikram's close associate. Their disagreement involved control, trust and the future of the empire.
- Vikram exploits Rajan's injury and death to seize the network.
- Bheeshma knows the full history but does not reveal everything immediately.
- Sheru's mother knew Rajan was alive but concealed it to protect Sheru.
- Jomon starts in Mahe and joins Sheru in Kochi.
- Sheru earns loyalty through decisions and action.
- The final story ends in Mahe. Free roam unlocks after credits.

# 6. ACT I — THE LITTLE LION

**Location:** Mahe  
**Tone:** 70% comedy, 30% emotion.

The player must enjoy Sheru's ordinary life. The juice shop is not a tutorial room that the story rushes past. It is the emotional home of the game.

## MISSION 1 — JUICE MASTER

**Type:** Main story mission  
**Location:** Mother's juice shop  
**Characters:** Sheru, Mother, first customer and background customers  
**Purpose:** Teach the juice-making gameplay loop and establish Sheru's humour.

### Opening cutscene

**EXT. MAHE STREET — MORNING**

A quiet morning. Shop shutters open. A bicycle passes. A vendor arranges fruit. From a small juice shop comes the sound of a blender.

**INT. JUICE SHOP — CONTINUOUS**

Sheru is wiping the counter. His mother is arranging fruit.

**MOTHER:** Sheru, counter clean aakkiyo?

**SHERU:** Clean aakki, amme.

Mother runs a finger across the counter and shows him the dust.

**MOTHER:** Ithaano clean aakkiyathu?

**SHERU:** Athu counter-inte original colour-aa. Njan maattan ninnilla.

Mother gives him a warning look.

**MOTHER:** Ravile thanne enne kondu parayippikkalle.

A customer enters.

**CUSTOMER:** Oru papaya juice.

**SHERU:** Da, healthy healthy!

**CUSTOMER:** Sugar venda, venda.

**SHERU:** Da, healthy kutta!

**CUSTOMER:** Kooda randu mutta puffs-um.

Sheru pauses and looks at the customer.

**SHERU:** Eda gomale... juice-il sugar venda, puffs-il mutta venam. Enthuvaada ee diet?

**CUSTOMER:** Athu cheat meal aanu.

**SHERU:** Cheat meal alla chetta, diet-ne konnu kuzhichu moodiyatha.

Mother tries not to laugh.

**MOTHER:** Sheru, mindaathe juice adikku.

**SHERU:** Njan mindaathirikkaam. Iyalude diet kandittu mixer polum karayunnundu.

### Gameplay

The player learns:
1. Select papaya from the fruit counter.
2. Cut and prepare the fruit.
3. Add the required ingredients.
4. Use the blender.
5. Pour the juice into a glass.
6. Serve the customer.
7. Collect payment.

If the player takes too long, the customer complains.

**CUSTOMER:** Mone, juice aano undaakkunnathu, papayayude jaathakam nokkuvaano?

**SHERU:** Jaathakam nokkiyittu nalla samayathe tharoo. Ippo grahanila shariyalla.

If the player adds sugar by mistake:

**CUSTOMER:** Njan sugar venda ennu paranjille?

**SHERU:** Paranjirunnu. Ente chevikk kettatha. Kaikku kurachu ormak kuravaa.

### Ending cutscene

Sheru collects the payment and wipes the counter.

**MOTHER:** Oru juice undaakkaan ithrayum neramo?

**SHERU:** Quality time edukum.

**MOTHER:** Enna ini muthal nee thanne kudicho.

Sheru looks at the next customer entering.

**SHERU:** Adutha aal varatte. Innathe laabham ningale kondu theerkkaan pattilla.

### Mission rewards
- Juice preparation unlocked.
- Shop interaction unlocked.
- Basic cash system introduced.

## MISSION 2 — JUICE DELIVERY

**Type:** Main story mission  
**Characters:** Sheru, Mother, Jomon, customers, shopkeepers and background NPCs  
**Purpose:** Introduce Jomon as a recurring companion and open the local area.

### Opening cutscene

**INT. JUICE SHOP — LATE MORNING**

Mother checks an order notebook.

**MOTHER:** Sheru, ee randu order-um koduthittu vaa. Onnu Lathayude kadayilum mattethu appurathe veettilum.

**SHERU:** Amme, kadayil vere aarum ille?

**MOTHER:** Neeyalle ullathu.

**SHERU:** Athaanu prashnam. Ee sthapanathinte muzhuvan bhaaravum ente cheruppam thaangunnilla.

Jomon enters.

**JOMON:** Njan help cheyyaam.

Sheru looks at him suspiciously.

**SHERU:** Nee enthinaa?

**JOMON:** Souhrudham.

**SHERU:** Kazhinja thavanayum souhrudham paranju ente 450 roopa kondupoyathalle?

**JOMON:** Athu friendship loan aanu.

**SHERU:** Palishayillatha loan alle? Enna thirichu tharaan enthinaa ithra thaamasam, thendi?

**MOTHER:** Randuperum poyi order koduthittu vaa. Vazhiyil ninnu chaayayum kudichu samayam kalayanda.

**JOMON:** Aunty, njan chaaya kudikkilla.

**SHERU:** Ivan chaaya kudikkilla. Kadayil free aayi kittunnathellaam kudikkum.

Mother hands them the delivery bag.

### Gameplay sequence

#### Delivery 1 — Residential lane

Sheru and Jomon reach the first customer. The customer checks the order.

**CUSTOMER 1:** Ithil sugar kuravaanallo?

**SHERU:** Ningal thanneyalle kurakkaan paranjathu?

**CUSTOMER 1:** Kurakkaan paranju. Ithrayum kurakkaan paranjilla.

**SHERU:** Appo sugar-inu oru minimum balance venamaayirunno?

Jomon turns away to hide his laughter.

The player confirms the order and collects payment.

#### Delivery 2 — Latha's grocery shop

Latha is arranging goods.

**LATHA:** Sheru, ithra vaikiyano varunnathu?

**SHERU:** Chechi, juice undaakkaan papayayodu aadyam sammatham vaanganam. Athinaanu thaamasam.

**LATHA:** Ninte naavu kurachaal ninakku nallathaa.

**SHERU:** Athinu kadayil vere saadhanam vaanganam chechi. Ippozhathe stock illa.

Latha asks about an old payment. Sheru checks the notebook.

**LATHA:** Ninte ammayodu parayanam, kazhinja aazhchathe paisa baakki undu.

**SHERU:** Chechi, ningalkku kadam kodukkaan ente ammakku bank license undo?

**LATHA:** Poyi ninte ammeye vilikku.

**SHERU:** Athaanu prashnam. Ammaye vilichaal juice-inoppam enneyum adichu tharum.

#### Tea-shop stop

Sheru and Jomon stop near Raghavan's tea shop.

**RAGHAVAN:** Enthadaa, innu randuperum jolikku irangiyo? Nattil entho valiya durantham nadanno?

**JOMON:** Chetta, njangal business valarthukayaanu.

**RAGHAVAN:** Aadyam ninte kadam valarthiyathu kurakku.

**SHERU:** Chetta, ivanu chaaya kodukkanda. Paisa kodukkilla. Glass maathram thirichu tharum.

**JOMON:** Nee ente friend aano shathruvaano?

**SHERU:** Paisa thirichu thannaal friend. Illenkil case.

### Ending cutscene

Sheru and Jomon return to the shop.

**MOTHER:** Ellam kodutho?

**SHERU:** Ellam koduthu.

**JOMON:** Njangal oru team aanu, aunty.

Mother looks into the nearly empty delivery bag.

**MOTHER:** Pinne ee oru glass evide?

Jomon looks at Sheru.

**JOMON:** Athu... vazhiyil oru cheriya prashnam.

**SHERU:** Prashnamalla. Ivan kudichu.

**JOMON:** Daahichu poyi.

**SHERU:** Ninte daahathinu kadayile stock muzhuvan vittaal mathiyallo, myre?

**MOTHER:** Randuperum akathu vaa. Aadyam glass kazhuku.

### Rewards
- Mahe neighbourhood exploration expanded.
- Jomon introduced as a companion.
- Delivery activity becomes repeatable.

## MISSION 4 — THE BLACK CAR

**Type:** Main story mission  
**Characters:** Sheru, Mother, Bheeshma's messengers/security team  
**Purpose:** Move from ordinary life to the family mystery.

### Opening cutscene

**EXT. JUICE SHOP — EVENING**

A black luxury Rolls-Royce stops outside. Two suited men step out. The street becomes quiet.

Sheru assumes they are customers.

**SHERU:** Juice venamengil parayu. Parking ivide aanu, showroom alla.

One man asks for Sheru.

**MAN:** Sheru Bharathan?

**SHERU:** Sheru mathi. Bharathan full-time use cheyyarilla.

Mother drops a glass.

The men explain that they have been searching for him. Mother steps between them and Sheru.

**MOTHER:** Ningalkku ivide entha vendathu?

**MAN:** Rajan saarinte makane kaananam.

Sheru turns to his mother.

**SHERU:** Aaraa Rajan?

Mother struggles to answer.

**MOTHER:** Mone... ninte achan.

**SHERU:** Ente achano? Ente achan marichille?

**MOTHER:** Sheru... ninte achan marichittilla.

Silence.

**SHERU:** Appo ithrayum kaalam njan achan illatha kutti aanennu karuthi nadannathu verutheyayirunno?

Mother cannot answer.

### Gameplay
The player can:
- Walk around the shop.
- Inspect the dropped glass.
- Speak to Mother.
- Decide when to enter the car.

Do not rush the revelation.

### Ending cutscene

Sheru looks back at the juice shop before entering the Rolls-Royce. Jomon arrives too late and sees the car leaving.

**JOMON:** Eda Sheru! Enneyum kondupoda!

The car disappears down the road.

## MISSION 6 — THE LAST KING

**Type:** Main story mission  
**Characters:** Sheru, Rajan, Bheeshma, doctor and security staff  
**Purpose:** Introduce Rajan and establish the emotional centre.

### Cutscene

**INT. RAJAN'S BEDROOM — NIGHT**

Rajan is badly injured and lying in bed. Medical equipment is nearby. Bheeshma stands at the doorway.

Sheru enters slowly.

Rajan looks at him for a long moment.

**RAJAN:** Sheru...

Sheru does not know what to say.

**SHERU:** Enne ariyamo?

Rajan tries to smile.

**RAJAN:** Ninne njan orikkalum marannittilla.

Sheru asks why Rajan left him and his mother.

**SHERU:** Enthinaa njangale vittupoyathu? Oru divasam polum achan evideyaanu ennu ariyaathe njan valarnnu.

Rajan explains that a dangerous gangster threatened the family. He believed leaving them was the only way to keep them safe. He could not return, but he watched Sheru from a distance.

**RAJAN:** Mone... enikku ninne orupaadu ishtamaanu. Ninn eyum ammeyum samrakshikkaanaanu njan annu poyathu. Oru gundayude bheeshani undayirunnu. Thirichu varaan enikku kazhinjilla. Pakshe doore ninnu njan ninne eppozhum nokkiyirunnu.

**SHERU:** Doore ninnu nokkiyaal achanaakumo?

Rajan is silent.

**RAJAN:** Illa. Njan ninte achan enna nilayil parajayappettu.

Sheru looks away. He is angry, hurt and overwhelmed.

Rajan asks Bheeshma to bring a small box.

Inside:
- A family photograph.
- Rajan's ring.
- A letter for Sheru.
- Documents connected to Rajan's legitimate businesses.

Rajan asks Bheeshma to protect Sheru, teach him who can be trusted and explain the empire.

**RAJAN:** Avane enne pole aakkanam ennulla. Pakshe avane avar thakarkkaan anuvadikkaruthu.

**BHEESHMA:** Ente jeevan ullidatholam njan avane samrakshikkum.

Rajan looks at Sheru.

# 15. DIALOGUE AND PERFORMANCE GUIDE

## Sheru

- Speaks in natural, colloquial Malayalam.
- Uses English words casually where a young Malayali would naturally use them.
- Sarcasm is his main weapon before he becomes physically powerful.
- Swears occasionally when annoyed, surprised or among close friends.
- Does not swear at his mother in a cruel or disrespectful way.
- Does not make jokes during every emotional beat.
- His late-game confidence comes from restraint, not from shouting.

## Bheeshma

- Speaks clearly and directly.
- Rarely swears.
- Rarely jokes.
- Shows affection through actions rather than speeches.
- Uses “Young Master” early.
- Calls him “Sheru” at the end.

## Jomon

- Talks too much.
- Makes ambitious plans.
- Gets frightened when plans go wrong.
- Uses humour to hide insecurity.
- Can swear casually with Sheru.
- Becomes brave when Sheru genuinely needs him.

## Mother

- Warm but strict.
- Does not treat Sheru like a king.
- Her humour is practical and grounded.
- Her scenes should feel emotionally honest.
- Avoid making her a constant scolding machine; show affection through small actions.

## Vikram

- Calm and controlled.
- Does not need to shout to threaten someone.
- Uses Sheru's inexperience against him.
- Believes his grievance justifies his actions.
- His insults should be cold rather than cartoonishly loud.

## Malayalam profanity and slang direction

Use colloquial Manglish slang only when it fits the relationship and situation. Examples of the register:
- “Myre” — close-friend frustration or a sharp outburst.
- “Thendi” — teasing or an angry insult.
- “Oola” — calling someone useless or ridiculous.
- “Patti” — harsher insult; use sparingly.
- “Kazhiveri” — angry or comedic insult.
- “Pottan” — lighter teasing.
- “Vaanam” — dismissive slang; use only where the character would naturally say it.

Do not stack swear words in every sentence. A well-timed “myre” is funnier than five random insults. Avoid slurs targeting protected groups. Do not make Mother or Bheeshma use the same slang pattern as Sheru and Jomon.

## Cinema and internet references

The game can have Malayalam mass-cinema energy and meme-like timing, but Sheru's dialogue should remain original. Do not copy long or recognisable lines from existing films, songs or social-media creators. Use original punchlines that feel like they belong to Sheru.

# 16. CONTINUITY AND PRODUCTION CHECKLIST

Before finalising a mission or cutscene, check:

1. Does the scene happen in the correct act and location?
2. Does every character know what they should know at this point?
3. Has Bheeshma been introduced before he appears in a mission?
4. Does Jomon's move from Mahe to Kochi have a clear reason?
5. Does Sheru's skill level match his experience at this point?
6. Does Vikram's behaviour escalate logically?
7. Does Sheru earn the trust of workers and associates?
8. Does each recovered business connect to one of Rajan's five pillars?
9. Does the mission add story, character, gameplay or world detail?
10. Does the scene leave enough room for serious emotion?
11. Are side missions optional unless explicitly part of the main plot?
12. Does the ending preserve Sheru's relationship with his mother?
13. Is the Malayalam dialogue natural when spoken aloud?
14. Does each character have a distinct rhythm and vocabulary?
15. Is the joke funny because of the character and situation, rather than just a swear word?

# FINAL NARRATIVE PRINCIPLE

Sheru's story is not simply about becoming the biggest gangster in Kochi.

It is the story of a boy who used to cut papaya in a small Mahe juice shop, listen to customer complaints and remind his friend to repay a loan. He discovers that his father built an empire, but also learns that the years his family lost while that empire was being built can never be returned.

He loses his father before he can truly know him. He finds a mentor in Bheeshma and a loyal friend in Jomon. He earns his place in a city that initially refuses to accept him.

He makes mistakes. He loses. He learns. He protects his people. Eventually, he builds an empire under his own name.

The world may call him **Sheru Bhai**.

But when he returns to his mother's juice shop, he is still her **mon** (her boy).

**Born in a juice shop. Raised by the streets. Destined for the throne.**
